What this does not do yet. It reads balances and the Transfer log. It does not yet price anything, so it cannot tell you profit and loss in ETH — that needs the swap decoding and the opening-tax read, which is the rest of stage 6. It shows what it can compute and stays quiet about the rest, rather than inventing a number.

Why the depth box. A full history scan of a chain at 58 million blocks is not something a browser should attempt. The default walks the last 250 000 blocks and splits the range whenever the node refuses it. Raise it and it takes longer; lower it and you see less.
